I'm a Senior Product Designer with 5+ years of experience in UX/UI and digital product across banking, fintech, e-commerce and real estate. I started as a graphic designer, moved into UX with a focus on accessibility and usability, and today I lead end-to-end projects, from product discovery to implementation.

I work side by side with product, engineering and business teams, mentor designers, and teach UX and portfolio design at the University of Buenos Aires and Universidad de San Andrés.

Soy Senior Product Designer con más de 5 años de experiencia en UX/UI y producto digital en banca, fintech, e-commerce y real estate. Empecé como diseñadora gráfica, me pasé a UX con foco en accesibilidad y usabilidad, y hoy lidero proyectos end-to-end, de product discovery a implementación.
